ReplyPilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Current status: ReplyPilot is an Instagram automation product under development and private testing. It is not currently open for public customer registration or paid subscriptions. This policy describes our current testing activities. We will update it before launching additional features.

1. Who operates ReplyPilot

ReplyPilot is a software project operated from India. For privacy questions, contact varadachiru@gmail.com.

2. What the product does

ReplyPilot is being developed to help authorized Instagram professional account owners configure and test automations related to comments on their Instagram posts. At present, the software is being tested with a developer-controlled Instagram account and a local development environment. Live automatic direct-message sending has not yet been enabled.

3. Information processed during testing

Depending on the test, we may access Instagram professional account identifiers and usernames, media identifiers and metadata, comment counts, and—when the Instagram API makes them available—comment identifiers, text, usernames, and timestamps. Our local prototype also stores campaign settings, simulated automation results, and activity logs in a local database. Authentication credentials and API access tokens may be used to make authorized requests to Meta's APIs.

4. Why we use information

We use this information to develop, debug, and test account connectivity, campaign matching, duplicate-comment handling, and future automation functionality. We do not currently offer automated message delivery to the public.

5. Services involved

Authorized Instagram data is obtained through Meta's official APIs, subject to Meta's platform terms and the permissions granted. Our public privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. The prototype application currently runs locally during development; hosting and service providers may change as the product develops. We will update this notice before expanding the service.

6. Storage and retention

During the current development phase, campaign and test-event data may be retained in a local development database until it is manually deleted or the test environment is reset. We have not yet established an automatic retention or deletion schedule. We intend to define production retention practices before customer launch.

7. Your choices and requests

If you believe information about you is being processed in ReplyPilot testing, or want to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, email varadachiru@gmail.com. We will review the request and respond as appropriate under applicable law and platform requirements. You can also manage third-party app access from Instagram's settings.

8. Security

We limit testing to authorized accounts and are working toward security controls appropriate for a public service. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send passwords or access tokens by email.

9. International processing

ReplyPilot is operated from India. Authorized data may be accessed from India during development. If we later offer the service internationally or use additional processors, we will update this policy and implement any applicable protections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

Email: varadachiru@gmail.com
