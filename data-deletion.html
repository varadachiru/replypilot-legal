ReplyPilot Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Current status: ReplyPilot is under development and private testing. Public registration and live automated Instagram message delivery are not yet available. These instructions cover requests relating to our current testing activities and will be updated before public launch.

How to request data deletion

  1. Send an email to varadachiru@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line ReplyPilot Data Deletion Request.
  3. Describe your relationship to ReplyPilot and provide only the information needed to locate your testing data, such as your Instagram username (if relevant) or the email you used to contact us.
  4. We may ask you to verify that you are authorized to request deletion of the relevant information before acting.

Do not email passwords, Instagram access tokens, app secrets, or government identity documents.

What happens after a request

We will review the request, identify any related data we control, and respond with the available deletion options and any verification required. Where deletion is applicable, we will handle it according to applicable law and explain any information that must be retained for legal or security reasons.

Because ReplyPilot is in private testing, deletion currently involves manual review. We are not claiming that an automated self-service deletion system exists.

Disconnecting Instagram access

You can review and remove an application's permissions through Instagram's settings for connected apps and websites, where supported. Revoking Instagram access prevents further authorized API access using that connection, but it may not by itself delete information previously stored by an application. Contact us using the email above for a deletion request.

Questions

For privacy or data deletion questions, contact varadachiru@gmail.com.
